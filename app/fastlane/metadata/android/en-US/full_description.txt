Divergram brings your dive community, logbook, maps, guides, and travel planning together.

Key features
- Share underwater photos, videos, reviews, and dive stories in Feed and Reels.
- Explore dive sites and resorts on the map.
- Log depth, temperature, time, buddy, equipment, and location.
- Read safety, equipment, skills, and marine-environment guides.
- Discover marine-life candidates and save observation records.
- Manage resort bookings, check-ins, training, and digital licenses.
- Customize and reorder your most-used bottom-menu features.
- Use notifications, messages, saved posts, stamps, membership, and donations.
- Use Korean, English, Japanese, or Chinese with light and dark themes.
- Enjoy adaptive layouts on Android foldables, tablets, landscape, and split-screen windows.

Divergram helps divers discover new places, keep organized records, and connect with the diving community.
