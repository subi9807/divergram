Divergram brings your dive community, logbook, maps, guides, and travel planning together.

Key features
- Feed and Reels for underwater photos, videos, reviews, and dive stories
- Dive-site map and resort discovery with location and facility details
- Dive logs for depth, temperature, time, buddy, equipment, and location
- Dive guides and education covering safety, equipment, skills, and marine environments
- Marine-life discovery and observation records
- Resort booking history and on-site check-in flows
- Digital diving license management
- Customizable bottom navigation for your most-used features
- Notifications, messages, saved posts, stamps, membership, and donations
- Korean, English, Japanese, and Chinese with light and dark themes
- Adaptive layouts for iPhone, iPad, Android foldables, tablets, landscape, and split-screen windows

Divergram helps divers discover new places, keep organized records, and connect with the diving community.
