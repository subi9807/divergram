## Equipment and safety cross-checks

The article was independently written after checking authoritative guidance that regulators and hoses should be inspected and function-tested before diving; a regulator check should include signs of cracking or damage, leaks or free flow, breathing and purge tests; buddy checks should confirm that the regulator works, the mouthpiece is secure and the alternate air source is functional and accessible; hoses should be monitored for leaks, cracking and changes in condition; and servicing should follow manufacturer guidance and be performed by qualified personnel:

- Divers Alert Network, Equipment: https://dan.org/safety-prevention/return-to-diving-safely/equipment/
- Divers Alert Network, Hose Failure: https://dan.org/alert-diver/article/hose-failure/
- Divers Alert Network, Gear Care Guidelines: https://dan.org/health-resource-type/smart-guides/
- Divers Alert Network, Pre-Dive Briefings: https://dan.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/pre-dive-safety-briefings.pdf
- Divers Alert Network, Maintain Your Gear: https://dan.org/alert-diver/article/maintain-your-gear/
- PADI, Basic Equipment Checks for Scuba Divers: https://blog.padi.com/basic-equipment-checks-for-scuba-divers/

The public guide does not teach regulator disassembly, improvised repair, gas-sharing technique or emergency ascent procedure. It directs divers to stop using suspect equipment, report rental-equipment defects, obtain qualified inspection, follow the manufacturer and recognized training for exact operation and maintenance, and end a dive using trained procedures if an abnormality appears underwater.
