## Safety cross-check

The article's statements about water immersion and wet clothing increasing heat loss, and about shivering, fatigue, reduced coordination, confusion, slurred speech and drowsiness as warning signs, were checked against official CDC and NOAA material. The public copy paraphrases general awareness principles, avoids diagnosis or treatment instructions and directs worsening symptoms to local emergency procedures and professional medical care.
