## Safety cross-checks

The article was independently written after reviewing authoritative guidance that insulated exposure suits can contribute to heat stress during hot surface activity; prolonged fully suited waiting, direct sun, humidity and exertion increase risk; shade, rest, hydration and delaying full suit closure can reduce exposure; dizziness, nausea, headache, weakness, cramps and poor concentration warrant stopping; and confusion, collapse or reduced responsiveness require emergency medical help:

The public guide does not diagnose heat illness, prescribe individual fluid quantities or teach untrained medical treatment. It directs divers to delay full suit closure, use shade and rest, report early symptoms, cancel entry after symptoms, activate the local emergency plan for serious warning signs, avoid forcing fluids into an altered person, and stay within trained first-aid limits.
