## Safety cross-checks

The article was independently written after reviewing authoritative guidance that alcohol can impair judgment, reaction and coordination; impairment can be underestimated; recent drinking can affect hydration and next-day readiness; and hangover symptoms can overlap with dive-related illness:

- Divers Alert Network, Drinking and Diving: Is it Safe?: https://dan.org/alert-diver/article/drinking-and-diving-is-it-safe/
- Divers Alert Network, A Timeline of Decompression Stress: https://dan.org/alert-diver/article/a-timeline-of-decompression-stress/
- Divers Alert Network, Uncertainty After Diving: https://dan.org/alert-diver/article/uncertainty-after-diving/
- PADI, Standard Safe Diving Practices: https://pros-blog.padi.com/standard-safe-diving-practices-for-both-scuba-divers-and-freedivers/
- British Sub-Aqua Club, Safe Diving Guide: https://www.bsac.com/safety/safe-diving-guide/search/?set=true&type=rfst
- British Sub-Aqua Club, Ocean Diver Dive Planning: https://www.bsac.com/document/ocean-diver-student-guide/1ocean-diver-student-guide-nov-2022-v2.1.pdf

The guide does not claim a universal safe number of drinks or clearance time, diagnose symptoms, or prescribe treatment. It directs divers not to drink before or between dives, to postpone when impairment or hangover symptoms are possible, to disclose concerns early, and not to dismiss new postdive symptoms as a hangover.
